So Far

The Journey

I started out learning the fundamentals of programming like most CSE students do, but game development is what pulled me in — the idea that code, art, and design could come together into something people actually play.

Since then I've split my time between engines like Godot and Unity, modeling and rendering in Blender, and keeping my problem-solving sharp through DSA. Every project — a game, a 3D model, a website — has taught me something I've carried into the next one.
